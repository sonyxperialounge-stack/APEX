   CAP checks at all (DOC-T06 caught it).

   summary used the word "telemetry"; the invariant scan (no analytics identifiers)
   correctly flagged `src/engines/doctor.ts`. Reworded to "outcome records"; the
   invariant stays absolute.

   stale/active locks but never judged lock-directory writability. Split into the three
   plan-named checks; writability rides the resolved home mode (READ_WRITE → OK,
   otherwise UNAVAILABLE) so a doctor check never performs a probe write.
