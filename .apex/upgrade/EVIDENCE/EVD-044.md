## What changed

`54 §11.1` (amending `13 §5`) — a two-stage size guard for any bulk read pulled into
context: <= 25% of the context budget expands freely, 25-50% warns and records the cost,
> 50% refuses with a narrower alternative.

- `src/engines/context-guard.ts` (new) — `decideAttachment`, `decideAttachmentForText`,
  `AttachPlan`; pure and deterministic (COR-005).
- `src/core/types.ts` — `ApexContextConfig { budgetTokens }`, `ApexConfig.context`.
- `src/engines/ledger.ts` — `DEFAULT_CONFIG.context`, nested-block normalisation, merge.
- `src/cli/archive-cli.ts` — `read` and `scroll` consult the guard before emitting events;
  refusal sets exit code 1 and suggests the narrower surface (`--around` window).
- `templates/config.json` — documented `context.budgetTokens` block.
- `core/08-CONTEXT.md` — "THE ATTACHED-CONTEXT GUARD" doctrine section.
- Tests: `test/engines/context-guard.test.ts` (new, 7 tests), `test/cli/archive-cli.test.ts`
  (+2: CTX-T07 hard refusal, soft-guard warn).
